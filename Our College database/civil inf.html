Civil Engineering Department

Government Polytechnic College

Student Information

The Civil Engineering Department provides quality technical education in surveying, construction technology, structural engineering, transportation, environmental engineering, and estimation. Students gain practical knowledge through laboratories, workshops, industrial visits, and project work to build successful careers in the construction industry.

Academic

The department follows the Diploma curriculum with experienced faculty, well-equipped laboratories, smart classrooms, library facilities, seminars, industrial training, and project-based learning. Students are encouraged to develop technical and professional skills through practical activities.

Admission

Admissions to the Civil Engineering Diploma course are offered according to the rules of the Department of Technical Education (DTE). Eligible students can apply through the admission process and complete document verification.
