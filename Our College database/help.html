🏫 Government Polytechnic Sira

📖 About the College

Government Polytechnic Sira is a government technical institution located in Sira, Tumakuru District, Karnataka. The college is committed to providing quality technical education through experienced faculty, modern laboratories, a well-equipped library, computer facilities, workshops, sports facilities, and a student-friendly learning environment. The institution focuses on developing technical knowledge, practical skills, innovation, discipline, leadership qualities, and career readiness for students.
