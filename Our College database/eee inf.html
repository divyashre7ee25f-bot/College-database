Electrical and Electronics Engineering Department

Government Polytechnic College

Student Information

The Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) Department provides students with knowledge in electrical machines, power systems, electronics, control systems, renewable energy, wiring, and industrial automation. Students gain practical experience through laboratories, workshops, industrial visits, and technical projects to prepare for successful careers in the electrical and electronics industry.

Academic

The department follows the Diploma curriculum prescribed by DTE. Students learn through classroom teaching, practical laboratory sessions, seminars, project work, internships, and industrial training with the guidance of experienced faculty members.

Admission

Admissions to the Diploma in Electrical and Electronics Engineering are conducted according to the Department of Technical Education (DTE) guidelines. Eligible candidates can complete the admission process after document verification.
