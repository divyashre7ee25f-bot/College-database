Food Processing & Preservation Department

Government Polytechnic College

Student Information

The Food Processing and Preservation Department provides students with knowledge in food science, food safety, food preservation techniques, quality control, packaging technology, and food product development. Students receive practical training through laboratories, industrial visits, and mini projects to prepare for careers in the food industry.

Academic

The department follows the Diploma curriculum prescribed by the Department of Technical Education (DTE). Students learn through classroom teaching, practical laboratory sessions, workshops, seminars, industrial training, and project work to improve technical and professional skills.

Admission

Admissions to the Diploma in Food Processing and Preservation are conducted according to DTE guidelines. Eligible students can apply through the admission process after completing the required document verification.
